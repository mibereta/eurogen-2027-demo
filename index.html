Welcome to EUROGEN 2027

Ideas that move intelligent design forward

We are delighted to invite researchers, engineers and industry practitioners to the 17th EUROGEN Conference in Kraków. EUROGEN 2027 brings together communities working on artificial intelligence, evolutionary and deterministic computing, design optimization and optimal control, with a strong focus on real-world applications.

The conference will take place from 26 to 28 October 2027 at Cracow University of Technology. Participants will have the opportunity to exchange new methods, benchmark ideas and practical experience across engineering, computational science and emerging AI-enabled design workflows.

17

A continuing European series

More than three decades of EUROGEN

EUROGEN began in 1995 and grew from the INGENET European Thematic Network on genetic algorithms. It has since become an established forum for advanced optimization techniques, including evolutionary algorithms, multi-objective optimization, deterministic methods and computational intelligence.

The conference has previously been held in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (1995), Trieste (1997), Jyväskylä (1999), Athens (2001), Barcelona (2003), Munich (2005), Jyväskylä (2007), Kraków (2009), Capua (2011), Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (2013), Glasgow (2015), Madrid (2017), Guimarães (2019), online (2021), Chania (2023) and Lahti (2025).

Scope

Research, methods and applications

EUROGEN is a thematic event under ECCOMAS, the European Community on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences. The conference connects fundamental advances with applications in science, technology and industry.

  • Artificial intelligenceData-driven models, machine learning and intelligent decision support.
  • Evolutionary methodsMetaheuristics and population-based approaches for complex design spaces.
  • Deterministic methodsGradient-based, adjoint, mathematical programming and optimal-control methods.
  • ApplicationsDesign, optimization and control across engineering and computational science.

Contribute to EUROGEN 2027

Sessions and individual papers

Researchers and technologists are invited to organize a Mini-Symposium (MS) or a Special Technical Session (STS). Organizers should propose a focused theme and a group of prospective speakers or presentations.

Authors may also submit individual abstracts through the open call, independently of any MS or STS. Accepted contributions will be grouped into thematic sessions coordinated by the conference organizers.

Submission formats

Two ways to take part

01

Mini-Symposia & Special Technical Sessions

A Mini-Symposium addresses novel methods and tools with a research-oriented focus. A Special Technical Session concentrates on innovative technologies and their industrial or societal applications.

  • Provide a title and a brief description of the proposed MS or STS.
  • Recommend four speakers; one may be identified as a keynote speaker.
  • Plan for a two-hour session.
  • Submit the proposal as a PDF using the conference template, to be published here.
02

Single papers

Individual contributions are welcome across all published conference topics. Submissions will be peer reviewed and arranged into coherent thematic sessions.

  • Prepare a one-page abstract using the EUROGEN 2027 template, to be published here.
  • Submit the abstract in PDF format.
  • Accepted abstracts are planned for inclusion in the online Book of Abstracts.
  • At least one author of each accepted contribution must register by the presenter deadline.

Travel to Kraków

By air

John Paul II International Airport Kraków–Balice (KRK) is approximately 11 km west of the city centre. The airport train reaches Kraków Główny in about 20 minutes. Regular public buses and official airport taxis are also available.

Kraków

A city of science, history and culture

Kraków is one of Poland’s principal academic and cultural centres. Its medieval Old Town, Wawel Castle, museums and lively neighbourhoods are within easy reach of the university campus.
